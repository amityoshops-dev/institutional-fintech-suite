1. Problem Statement & Regulatory Context

Commercial B2B settlement flows frequently violate compliance audits when statutory tax liabilities (GST) are co-mingled with supplier payables. Under RBI Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways, all incoming commercial funds must be pooled into dedicated nodal/escrow accounts with immediate automated isolation of statutory taxes into non-operating escrow sub-ledgers prior to supplier settlement.

1. Inbound Ingress ERP webhook dispatches invoice payload; gateway verifies counterparty GSTIN and risk status.
2. Atomic Sequestration 18% tax component is isolated into non-operating escrow; net balance cleared to supplier.
3. Float & Reconciliation Corporate treasury captures float yield until statutory tax clearance; zero manual reconciliation.
