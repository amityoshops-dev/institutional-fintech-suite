Document Spec: BR-DPI-2026-v2 | Domain: RBI PA/PG Nodal Accounting & DPI Rails

1. Problem Statement & Regulatory Context

Commercial B2B settlement flows regularly fail audit compliance because tax liabilities (GST) are co-mingled with supplier payables. Under RBI Guidelines on Payment Aggregators (PAs), all collected funds must reside in pooled nodal accounts with immediate automated isolation of statutory taxes into non-operating escrow accounts.

2. Architecture & Data Flow

ERP (Frappe / ERPNext) --[M2M REST Webhook]--> BharatRails Ingress Gateway (Port 8080) ├── Double-Entry Engine: │ ├── Debits: ACC-CUSTOMER-RECEIVABLE (Grand Total) │ ├── Credits: ACC-GST-ESCROW (18% Statutory Tax Isolated) │ └── Credits: ACC-SUPPLIER-PAYABLE (Net Commercial Value) ├── Account Aggregator (Setu Sandbox / Sahamati Rail): │ └── Extracts 180-Day Cash Flow Profile --> ULI Underwriting Engine └── Graph Risk Engine: └── Monitors Transaction Fan-Out --> Applies Automated Lien Freezes

3. Monetization & Commercial Mechanics

  • Treasury Float Yield: Interest yield generated on aggregated GST held in nodal escrow pending statutory tax clearance.
  • Basis-Point Take Rate: 15-45 bps charged on gross settled B2B invoice volume.
  • Lending Spread (ULI/LSP): 1.5-2.0% origination fee on automated working capital sanctioned via AA statement analysis.
